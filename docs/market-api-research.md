## Notes that are in the docs (not extra research)

- **DNSE** market-data REST limits above are the “Normal Rate Limits” table. WS connection max lifetime: **8 hours**. Ping every **3 minutes**.
- **Vnstock** free vs sponsor also differs by **source** (community: KBS, VCI, MSN crawlers; sponsor: MAS, KBS, VCI, VND, Binance, Dukascopy, etc.). That is quality/stability, not a coverage count.
- **FiinQuant** [Gói & Chi phí](https://fiinquant.vn/Pricing) repeats several comparison tables with **different** connection counts and historical depths. This file copies the first full “Dữ liệu giao dịch” table for the free vs Cơ Bản rows and flags conflicts instead of picking a winner.
- **Simplize** SimX pages scanned do not define a free tier vs a paid tier, so there is a **single** SimX row.
